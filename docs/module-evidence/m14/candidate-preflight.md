# M14 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M14's current
workbook, six-session change sequence, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M14's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source/design/teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M14's six connected sessions; change pressure and
preserved observations; design, state, failure, Git, and review evidence;
prediction before reveal; confidence-aware diagnostic and repair route; spaced
retrieval; change dossier and rubric; accessible diagram metadata; supportive
oral-defense protocol; Study Partner prompt; and shared Mermaid accessibility
regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the Arc III source map and
M14 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect software-design, refactoring,
subtyping, Git, review, cost, visual, and source/reuse claims; real
keyboard/screen-reader and chat-whiteboard experience; psychological safety;
and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or
publication claim changes.
