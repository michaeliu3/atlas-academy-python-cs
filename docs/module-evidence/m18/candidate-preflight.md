# M18 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M18's current
workbook, six-session operating-systems sequence, source ledger, accessible
Mermaid reader surface, bounded reference model, and module-scoped companion.
It does not change M18's `legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open`
learner availability, or unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an executed operating-systems experiment, an exact
source commit to a successful CI run, a human source/systems/teaching review,
a private deployment record, a security clearance, a release, a publication
decision, an oral-conversation result, a learner record, or learner mastery.
Those remain separate evidence questions; the release criterion stays
explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M18's six connected sessions; resource ownership,
process/lifecycle, virtual-memory, file/authority, shutdown/recovery, and
publication evidence; code-reading/debugging/design; prediction before reveal;
confidence-aware diagnostic and repair route; spaced retrieval; the durable
worker evidence dossier and acceptance invariants; accessible diagram metadata;
supportive oral-defense protocol; Study Partner prompt; bounded reference-model
tests; and shared Mermaid accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is
limited to the M18 source map and M18 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect operating-systems, process,
virtual-memory, portability, authority, shutdown, recovery, source/reuse,
visual, and accessibility claims; real keyboard/screen-reader and
chat-whiteboard experience; psychological safety; and exact source-commit
CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
