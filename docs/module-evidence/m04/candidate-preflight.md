# M4 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M4's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M4's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M4's six connected sessions, formal derivation from an
informal policy to a quantified predicate, prediction before reveal,
proof/probability boundary work, code-reading and debugging studios,
confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval, prerequisite-domain portfolio,
accessible graph text alternative, supportive oral-defense route, and Mermaid
accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the curriculum
source map and the M4 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect Python-language claims, mathematical
arguments, source authority and reuse terms, real keyboard/screen-reader
experience, psychological safety, and exact source-commit CI/deployment
provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
