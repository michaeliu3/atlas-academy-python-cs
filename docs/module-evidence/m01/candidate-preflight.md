# M1 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M1's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M1's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M1's six connected sessions, object/binding
derivation, delayed-alias code-reading comparison, prediction before reveal,
confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval, event-log dossier, accessible
diagram text, supportive oral-defense route, and Mermaid accessibility
regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the curriculum source map
and the M1 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect Python-language claims, source
authority and reuse terms, real keyboard/screen-reader experience,
psychological safety, and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before
any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
