# M9 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M9's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M9's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M9's six connected sessions, ordered-operation and
invariant derivation, tree/heap/sort/trie proof ideas, counterexamples,
bounded workload observation, code-reading/debugging/design work, prediction
traces, confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval, constructive evidence
dossier, accessible diagram text, supportive oral-defense route, Study Partner
prompt, and Mermaid accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is
limited to the Arc II source map and the M9 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect mathematical, Python-semantic,
CPython, Unicode/normalization, and workload claims; source authority and
reuse terms; real keyboard/screen-reader and whiteboard experience;
psychological safety; and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before
any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
