# M35 authoring-only candidate evidence preflight

## Authoring candidate boundary

This document records an M35 **candidate-only structural evidence package**.
It binds the hidden machine-learning and representation workbook draft, its
instructor-facing source research, its six-session/output topology, its module
companion, and a bounded shared ML signal-routing fixture/test to inspectable
local paths. It does not change M35's canonical state: M35 remains
`authoring-only`, `hidden` from the reader, and unreleased.

Nothing here adds M35 to the learner manifest, opens a route, creates a
learner-facing studio, turns its instructor source research into the canonical
graph source map, approves learner delivery, or changes the graph's release
tuple.

## What this candidate package can establish

- It declares grounded anchors for all eighteen module-contract criteria in
  the hidden M35 draft, including prerequisite repair, six sessions,
  representation/evaluation assumptions, visual prose alternatives,
  diagnostic/retrieval draft, dossier, oral-defense prompts, companion handoff,
  and bounded reference fixtures/tests.
- The hidden delivery map checks that each declared session has a visible typed
  local output and that prerequisite-bridge artifacts appear in their first
  consuming sessions. It is an authoring topology check, not a learner-delivery
  decision or quality review.
- The source material is explicitly instructor-facing candidate research. It
  provides source, claim, assumption, counterexample, and reuse planning
  context; it is not a canonical source-map binding or completed learner-facing
  source review.
- The shared fixture/test makes small synthetic traces inspectable. It is not
  an interactive learner studio, a population claim, a browser test, or
  evidence that CI has run for any candidate commit.

## Explicit nonclaims

- This dossier does not establish mathematical, statistical, pedagogical,
  source, license, visual, accessibility, browser, oral-defense, or interaction
  quality of the draft.
- No learner has thereby entered M35, completed its draft sessions, passed an
  oral defense, demonstrated mastery, or created a portfolio artifact.
- This dossier does not bind a source commit to successful CI, source review,
  private deployment, security review, release record, or publication.
- It does not claim any Codex Teaching Assistant or Study Partner chat used
  voice, a particular model setting, a rendered whiteboard, or automatic
  Notion capture.

## Required path before promotion

1. Keep M35 authoring-only and hidden. The existing fixed selector at
   `content/course/contracts/review-candidates/m35.v1.json` already freezes
   `content/modules/35_machine_learning_representation.md` and its candidate
   source ledger for qualified review. It is not the graph-bound learner source
   map, an approval, or promotion authority; the separate `content/authoring`
   workbook and authoring-delivery map remain authoring evidence only.
2. Conduct explicit independent review on a preserved review-ready commit of
   eight lenses: claims/derivations/counterexamples; source/license links;
   visual and semantic accessibility; diagnostic and retrieval quality;
   dossier/rubric quality; bounded fixture or interaction behavior; TA/Study
   Partner oral/whiteboard flow; and learner privacy/record control.
3. Add a learner manifest/route only after the reviewed contract, tests,
   accessibility checks, and release requirements are satisfied; preserve the
   exact reviewed commit as an ancestor.
4. Collect exact-source-commit CI, source-review, private-deployment,
   limitations, changelog, and provenance evidence in later additive records
   before describing M35 as published or released.
