# M27 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M27’s current
workbook, typed six-session packet, source ledger, studio, bounded reference
model, and companion record. It does not change M27’s `legacy-baseline`
contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or unrecorded release
state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, or
learner mastery. Those remain separate evidence questions; the release
criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires the M27 workbook’s six connected sessions, formal
claim-to-countermodel progression, visible formula-versus-enumeration
experiment, confidence-aware diagnostic, retrieval queue, evidence dossier,
and supportive oral-defense protocol. Its source-ledger scope is limited to
the canonical M27 source map and M27 source-audit addendum. Its studio and
reference-model checks are bounded local teaching artifacts, not theorem,
browser, accessibility, CI, deployment, or production-system guarantees.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect mathematical correctness, source
authority and license interpretation, accessibility in real browser and
assistive-technology contexts, learning quality, and the exact source-commit
CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
