# M15 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M15's current
workbook, six-session durable-artifact sequence, source ledger, accessible
Mermaid reader surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M15's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source/packaging/teaching review, a clean-install result, a private
deployment record, a security clearance, a release, a publication decision, an
oral-defense result, a live chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery.
Those remain separate evidence questions; the release criterion stays
explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M15's six connected sessions; representation, resource,
schema, trust, artifact, and rollback boundaries; code-reading/debugging/design;
prediction before reveal; confidence-aware diagnostic and repair route; spaced
retrieval; durable artifact dossier and rubric; accessible diagram metadata;
supportive oral-defense protocol; Study Partner prompt; and shared Mermaid
accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the Arc III
source map and M15 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect Python, packaging, artifact,
clean-install, filesystem, dependency, security, rollback, visual, and
source/reuse claims; real keyboard/screen-reader and chat-whiteboard
experience; psychological safety; and exact source-commit CI/deployment
provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
