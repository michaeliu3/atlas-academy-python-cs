# M7 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M7's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M7's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M7's six connected sessions, access-law derivation,
iterator/generator prediction traces, bounded-buffer proof idea,
code-reading/debugging/design work, confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced
retrieval, constructive evidence dossier, accessible diagram text,
supportive oral-defense route, and Mermaid accessibility regression. Its

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect Python-language and concurrency
boundary claims, source authority and reuse terms, real keyboard/screen-reader
and whiteboard experience, psychological safety, and exact source-commit
CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
