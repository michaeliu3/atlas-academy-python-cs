# M10 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M10's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M10's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M10's six connected sessions, graph-question
derivation, representation invariant, shortest-path proof idea,
counterexamples, bounded numerical experiment, code-reading/debugging/design,
prediction before reveal, confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval,
graph-planner dossier, accessible diagram text, supportive oral-defense route,
Study Partner prompt, and shared Mermaid accessibility regression. Its source-ledger
scope is limited to the Arc II source map and M10 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect graph-theoretic, algorithmic,
Python-container, complexity, measurement, visual, and source/reuse claims;
real keyboard/screen-reader and whiteboard experience; psychological safety;
and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or
publication claim changes.
