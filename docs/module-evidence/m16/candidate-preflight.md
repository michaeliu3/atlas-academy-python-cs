# M16 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M16's current
workbook, six-session relational-systems sequence, source ledger, accessible
Mermaid reader surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M16's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind a live engine/configuration to a successful CI run,
a human source/database/teaching review, a private deployment record, a
security clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result,
a live chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M16's six connected sessions; relation/FD/key,
constraint/result, plan, transaction/retry, recovery/restore evidence;
code-reading/debugging/design; prediction before reveal; confidence-aware
diagnostic and repair route; spaced retrieval; reconciliation dossier and
acceptance invariants; accessible diagram metadata; supportive oral-defense
protocol; Study Partner prompt; and shared Mermaid accessibility regression.

source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect relational, SQL, engine/configuration,
plan, transaction, recovery, backup, visual, and source/reuse claims; real
keyboard/screen-reader and chat-whiteboard experience; psychological safety;
and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or
publication claim changes.
