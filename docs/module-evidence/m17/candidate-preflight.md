# M17 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M17's current
workbook, six-session computer-architecture sequence, source ledger,
accessible Mermaid reader surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not
change M17's legacy-baseline contract state, legacy-open learner availability,
or unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind a successful architecture experiment, a human
source/architecture/teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M17's six connected sessions; representation, state,
ISA, microarchitecture, hierarchy, execution-stack, and bounded-observation
evidence; code-reading/debugging/design; prediction before reveal;
confidence-aware diagnostic and repair route; spaced retrieval; the architecture
evidence dossier and acceptance invariants; accessible diagram metadata;
supportive oral-defense protocol; Study Partner prompt; and shared Mermaid
accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the M17 source
map and M17 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect architecture, ISA, Python/CPython,
performance, timing, source/reuse, visual, and accessibility claims; real
keyboard/screen-reader and chat-whiteboard experience; psychological safety;
and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or
publication claim changes.
