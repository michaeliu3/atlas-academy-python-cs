# M11 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M11's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader surface,
and module-scoped companion. It does not change M11's `legacy-baseline`
contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M11's six connected sessions; strategy formulation;
definitions, assumptions, proof ideas, counterexamples, and a bounded numerical
comparison; code-reading/debugging/design; prediction before reveal;
confidence-aware diagnostic and repair route; spaced retrieval; strategy dossier
and rubric; accessible diagram metadata; supportive oral-defense protocol;
Study Partner prompt; and shared Mermaid accessibility regression. Its
source-ledger scope is limited to the Arc II source map and M11 source-audit
addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect algorithmic, complexity, probability,
approximation, Python, measurement, visual, and source/reuse claims; real
keyboard/screen-reader and whiteboard experience; psychological safety; and
exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or publication
claim changes.
