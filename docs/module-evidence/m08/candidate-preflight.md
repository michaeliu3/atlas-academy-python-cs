# M8 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M8's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M8's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M8's six connected sessions, lookup-to-index
derivation, collision/equality traces, key-contract counterexample, qualified
cost derivation, bounded collision experiment, code-reading/debugging/design
work, confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval, inverted-index dossier,
accessible diagram text, supportive oral-defense route, Study Partner prompt,
and Mermaid accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to
the Arc II source map and the M8 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect algorithmic, Python-semantic,
CPython, security, proof, and measurement claims; source authority and reuse
terms; real keyboard/screen-reader and whiteboard experience; psychological
safety; and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle
or publication claim changes.
