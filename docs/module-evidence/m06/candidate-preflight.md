# M6 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M6's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M6's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M6's six connected sessions, representation definition
and model boundary, indexed-access derivation, linked-insertion
counterexample, bounded numerical experiment, code-reading/debugging/design,
prediction before reveal, confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval,
representation dossier, accessible diagram text, supportive oral-defense route,
Study Partner prompt, and Mermaid accessibility regression. Its source-ledger
scope is limited to the M6 source-audit addendum and the learner-facing links it
qualifies.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect mathematical, Python-semantic,
CPython, locality, and measurement claims; source authority and reuse terms;
real keyboard/screen-reader and whiteboard experience; psychological safety;
and exact source-commit CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or
publication claim changes.
