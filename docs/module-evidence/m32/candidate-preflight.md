# M32 authoring-only candidate evidence preflight

## Authoring candidate boundary

This document records an M32 **candidate-only structural evidence package**.
It binds the hidden Systems Languages, Scientific Python & Accelerators workbook
draft, its instructor-facing source research, its six-session/output topology,
its module companion, a bounded JavaScript authoring fixture/test, and one
explicit NumPy 2.3.5 CPU-only observation with its scoped unittest source to
inspectable local paths. It does not change M32's canonical state: M32 remains
`authoring-only`, `hidden` from the reader, and unreleased.

Nothing here adds M32 to the learner manifest, opens a route, creates a
learner-facing studio, turns its instructor source research into the canonical
graph source map, approves learner delivery, or changes the graph's release
tuple.

## What this candidate package can establish

- It declares grounded anchors for all eighteen module-contract criteria in
  the hidden M32 draft, including prerequisite retrieval, six sessions,
  first-principles boundaries, code-reading and debugging tasks, a scoped
  NumPy observation, visual prose alternatives, diagnostic/retrieval draft,
  dossier, oral-defense prompts, companion handoff, and bounded fixtures.
- The hidden delivery map checks that each declared session has a visible typed
  local output and that prerequisite-bridge artifacts appear once in their
  canonical first-consuming sessions. It is an authoring topology check, not a
  learner-delivery decision or quality review.
- The source research is explicitly instructor-facing candidate planning
  material. It provides source, claim, assumption, counterexample, and reuse
  planning context; it is not a canonical source-map binding or a completed
  learner-facing source review.
- The deterministic fixture/test and fixed NumPy observation make small M32
  examples inspectable. They are not an interactive learner studio, a GPU or
  framework trace, a general benchmark, a browser test, or evidence that CI
  has run for any candidate commit.

## Explicit nonclaims

- This dossier does not establish the systems, mathematical, pedagogical,
  source, license, visual, accessibility, browser, oral-defense, or interaction
  quality of the draft.
- The NumPy observation does not establish an array-library standard,
  buffer-protocol behavior, GPU behavior, benchmark result, portability claim,
  or a general allocation/copy conclusion.
- No learner has thereby entered M32, completed its draft sessions, passed an
  oral defense, demonstrated mastery, or created a portfolio artifact.
- This dossier does not bind a source commit to a successful CI run, source
  review, private deployment, security review, release record, or publication.
- It does not claim any Codex Teaching Assistant or Study Partner chat used
  voice, a particular model setting, a rendered whiteboard, or automatic Notion
  capture.

## Required path before promotion

1. Keep M32 authoring-only and hidden until a reviewed delivery design supplies
   a separate fixed hidden review-candidate selector, canonical learner source
   ledger, and appropriate learner-facing interaction or equivalent, without
   weakening prerequisite gates. That selector must not reuse this authoring
   workbook or its authoring-delivery map as promotion authority.
2. Conduct explicit independent review of the workbook, source/claim/license
   boundaries, CPU-observation scope, visual and accessibility behavior,
   diagnostic/rubric quality, and the TA/Study Partner/oral-defense flow on a
   preserved review-ready commit.
3. Add a learner manifest/route only after the reviewed contract, tests,
   accessibility checks, and release requirements are satisfied; preserve the
   exact reviewed commit as an ancestor.
4. Collect exact-source-commit CI, source-review, private-deployment,
   limitations, changelog, and provenance evidence in later additive records
   before describing M32 as published or released.
