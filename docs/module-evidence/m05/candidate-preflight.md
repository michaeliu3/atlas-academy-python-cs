# M5 candidate-only structural preflight

## Candidate boundary

This document records a structural candidate preflight for M5's current
workbook, six-session packet, source ledger, accessible Mermaid reader
surface, and module-scoped companion. It does not change M5's
`legacy-baseline` contract state, `legacy-open` learner availability, or
unrecorded release state.

The candidate does not bind an exact source commit to a successful CI run, a
human source or teaching review, a private deployment record, a security
clearance, a release, a publication decision, an oral-defense result, a live
chat session, a Notion write, or learner mastery. Those remain separate
evidence questions; the release criterion stays explicitly open.

## Structural scope

The candidate requires M5's six connected sessions, cost-model derivation,
code-reading and cost-mismatch debugging, prediction before reveal,
confidence-aware diagnostic, spaced retrieval, representation decision dossier,
accessible diagram text, supportive oral-defense route, and Mermaid
accessibility regression. Its source-ledger scope is limited to the curriculum
source map and the M5 source-audit addendum.

## Later review questions

A future human review must still inspect mathematical and Python-performance
claims, source authority and reuse terms, real keyboard/screen-reader and
whiteboard experience, psychological safety, and exact source-commit
CI/deployment provenance before any lifecycle or publication claim changes.
