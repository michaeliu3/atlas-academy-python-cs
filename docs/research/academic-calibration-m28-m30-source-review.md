## Cross-module coherence and genuine gaps

The prerequisite spine is coherent: M27 supplies proof and quantifier discipline; M28 supplies linear/finite-precision representation; M29 supplies limits, local approximation, integration, and convergence; M30 turns those into probability, sampling, and inference claims. This tracks the prerequisite relationships visible in Stanford CS109 and Georgia Tech mathematics/data-analysis routes without falsely turning separate university courses into one credential.

**No learner-blocking content or evidence gap was found in M28–M30 for the stated 60-day core.** Each has a visible prerequisite/forward route; six connected sessions; definitions and assumptions; proof ideas/counterexamples; numerical or code-reading evidence where relevant; a diagnostic; a dossier; and a constructive oral-defense route.

The following are **intentional scope boundaries, not defects**:

- M28 is not a complete numerical-linear-algebra algorithms course.
- M29 is not a full proof-based real-analysis, ODE, or PDE sequence.
- M30 is not a complete measure-theory, causal-inference, Bayesian-computation, or nonparametric-statistics sequence.
