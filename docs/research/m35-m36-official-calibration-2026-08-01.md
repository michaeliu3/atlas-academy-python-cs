# M35–M36 official calibration — 2026-08-01

**Scope.** A bounded authoring review of the hidden M35 and M36 workbooks,
their source research, delivery maps, companions, and non-promoting evidence
records. It is not a learner-route, source-license, accessibility, human-review,
or release decision. Both modules remain `authoring-only` and hidden.

## Verdict

The drafts are strong, connected **authoring candidates**: M35 moves from
representation and comparable baselines through evaluation, training evidence,
debugging, and authority; M36 moves from risk notation through finite-class
theorem scope, numerical execution, shift/monitoring, and a bounded dossier.
They already contain original explanations, prediction-before-reveal, code
reading, counterexamples, diagnostics, retrieval, a dossier, constructive oral
defense, and module-specific TA/Study Partner prompts. They are **not yet
study-ready learner modules**: no reviewed learner-facing pack, canonical
claim-linked source map, rendered accessibility/delivery review, learner pilot,
or release/provenance evidence exists. The current authoring-only boundary is
therefore correct.

## Release truth

The shared deterministic fixture is a useful **bounded reference artifact**;
it is not a learner studio, framework validation, generalization proof, or
reliability certificate. A real learner-facing interaction can remain lean:
one rendered reading/whiteboard flow plus the original fixed traces and a
constructive oral-defense pilot is enough to review the delivery model before
considering any portal release. No current evidence supports a claim of
university-equivalent enrollment, mastery, published status, or deployment
fitness.
