# M25/M26 primary-source calibration follow-up — 2026-08-03

## Scope and truth boundary

This compact, research-only check compares the existing gated M25/M26
workbooks with official course material. It makes **no** learner-facing,
availability, review, deployment, credit, mastery, or publication change:
M25/M26 remain preview/gated and M31–M36 remain authoring-only. Sources were
rechecked on 2026-08-03 and are comparison anchors, not material to copy.

## Confirmed alignment and one scoped gap

| Atlas focus | Official calibration | Result |

A written draft, green check, or candidate prompt remains insufficient evidence of a live review/defense. |

Its external-track selection wording, however, does not yet explicitly require evidence that a chosen project is active or that the task corresponds to a real tracked need. That omission can make a stewardship exercise artificial or unreviewable. |

## Lean reweave implication

When M26 is eligible for its later, one focused reweave, add a small
**external-track selection check** rather than a new system: record (1) recent
project activity and a contribution route, (2) a real issue/request or a clear
reason to use the Atlas-local track instead, and (3) the maintainer/community
boundary. Do **not** require public submission, a merge, community contact, or
external data. A private local reproducer/review remains the default safe
alternative when any of those facts are unavailable.

The remaining release-time evidence gap is operational rather than authoring:
a qualified, voluntary M26 TA conversation must actually exercise the
changed-premise defense and review a dossier before anyone describes the oral
flow as learner-delivered. That future interaction must stay non-grading,
privacy-conscious, and distinct from institutional peer, staff, or maintainer
feedback.
