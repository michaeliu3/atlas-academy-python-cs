## Remaining work before the goal can be claimed complete

1. Observe platform-specific voice, rendering, Notion-save, and deletion
   behavior only when the learner chooses to use the designated chats. Keep
   actual observations separate from material readiness; never store learner
   content or claim an unobserved write.
2. Before any portal M31–M36 publication decision, perform a focused
   source/reuse and rendered-accessibility review of the selected candidate;
   correct only a concrete issue rather than adding infrastructure to mark a
   checklist.
3. Retain the evidence-gated private M25/M26 route or promote selected material
   to a learner-visible portal stage; either decision must preserve honest
   graph, source, accessibility, and release language.
4. Record one normally requested reviewed full gate when the final content
   batch is ready for review; do not manually dispatch CI or change PR state
   merely to manufacture evidence.
5. Conduct the final requirement-by-requirement audit against the chosen
   private-versus-portal delivery boundary.

## Deliberately deferred, not silently missing

- Formal publication/release certification for every legacy module.
- Portal-controlled voice, model-quality, equation-rendering, or Notion-write
  claims that require platform evidence.
- A public multi-user backend, additional dashboards, or an interactive studio
  for every module.
- Equating this private course with university enrollment, credit, a degree, or
  universal mastery.
